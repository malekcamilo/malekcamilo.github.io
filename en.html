About Me

Machine Learning Professor at the National University of Luján (UNLu), with postgraduate studies in data intelligence and natural language processing (NLP). My academic activity focuses on open language models and their adaptation to specific domains.

My thesis work, “Efficient Adaptation Strategies for Open Language Models”, compares full fine-tuning (FFT), PEFT techniques (LoRA, QLoRA, DoRA, and QDoRA), and GaLore on Transformer-based models, through experiments in the Clementina XXI HPC environment.

I also have over 25 years of IT experience, especially in the administration and optimization of Oracle databases, networks, and Linux/Windows systems.
